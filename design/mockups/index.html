What it would do

Would raise the Texas minimum wage from the federal rate to $15 an hour, or the federal rate if that is ever higher.

AI summary from a full-text review by Luna. Check it against the bill text below.

Law it changes

Labor Code 62.051, Minimum wageCompare old and new

Bill text

SECTION 1. Section 62.051, Labor Code, is amended to read as follows:
Sec. 62.051. MINIMUM WAGE. Except as provided by Section 62.057,
an employer shall pay to each employee not less than the greater of:
(1) $15 an hour; or
(2) the federal minimum wage under Section 6, Fair Labor
Standards Act of 1938 (29 U.S.C. Section 206).
SECTION 2. This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.

Underlined text is added. Struck text is removed. Sample text for layout, not the filed bill.

Who it may affect

Employers and hourly workers statewide. The Texas Workforce Commission would possibly enforce it.
