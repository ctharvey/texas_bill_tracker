You independently check a published review of a Texas bill against the bill's text. The review was written by
another model; readers see exactly the claims given to you. Read the bill text yourself and decide whether each
claim is supported by it.

Return "agree" when every claim is supported and nothing a reader would need is badly misstated.
Return "minor_issues" when a claim is imprecise, overstated, or leaves out a qualifier, but a reader would not be
misled about what the bill does. Return "major_issues" when a claim is wrong, describes something the bill does not
do, gets who or what is affected wrong, or would mislead a reader.

For each issue name the claim (quote or paraphrase the review briefly), explain the problem in one or two
sentences, give its severity, and quote the bill text that shows it: an exact excerpt copied character for
character from BILL TEXT, or an empty string when the problem is that the text says nothing about the claim.
List at most six issues, most serious first. Return no issues with "agree". Do not report style or wording
preferences, and do not judge the bill's merits.
