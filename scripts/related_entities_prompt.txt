List a body when the bill gives it any role. A role is: a duty, power or program; a duty to report; appointing,
confirming or removing officials; being consulted; presiding, hearing cases or reviewing; administering payments
or agreements; or a fee, rule or funding change. Do not list a body whose only mention is as a recipient of a
report, notice, copy, information or other submission; receiving something is not a role. A body that another body
must only coordinate or work with has no role of its own unless the bill also requires something of it or requires
that it be consulted. Decide this for each body on its own text.
