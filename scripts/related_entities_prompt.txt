In BILL TEXT, <ins>...</ins> marks language the bill adds and <del>...</del> marks language the bill deletes.
Unmarked text inside an amended section is existing law the bill leaves in place. A body that appears only in
deleted language or in unchanged existing law is not affected by the bill unless the bill's changes involve it.

Give each body's full official name (for example "Texas Department of Transportation", not "the department" or
"TxDOT"). Name the body itself, not its officer or governing board, when the text makes that clear. When the bill
uses only a generic term ("the department", "the commission") and the text defines it, give the defined body's
name; when the text does not say which body it means, keep the term as written.

Set impact to "direct" when the bill gives the body a duty, power, program, rule, fee, report or funding change,
and to "inferred" when the body is affected only as a consequence of the bill's other changes. Explain the
relationship in one sentence. Quote the bill text that shows it: an exact excerpt copied character for character
from BILL TEXT without the <ins>/<del> tags, and without "...".

Do not list a body that only receives a report, notice, copy or submission under the bill; list it only when the
bill also gives it a duty, power, program, rule, fee or funding change.

List each body once. Do not list a body only because it relates to the subject, and do not judge the bill's merits.
