Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Texas Bill Atlas is a free, independent, open-source project. We believe you should be able to research legislation without creating an account or providing personal information.

Information we collect

We do not require registration, names, email addresses, or other personal information to use the service.

We do not sell personal information, serve targeted advertising, or intentionally track individual browsing activity.

Analytics and security

We use Cloudflare for website delivery, security, and traffic analytics.

Cloudflare may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, request information, and website usage statistics. Cloudflare may also use cookies necessary for security and abuse prevention.

For more information, see Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Watchlists and saved preferences

Watchlists and preferences are stored locally in your browser by default.

If you choose to enable watchlist synchronization, we store the selected bill numbers, the member codes of legislators you follow, your saved searches (their names, search words and filters), filters, and hashed access keys on our server. View-only links show the bill numbers only.

No account or personal information is required. Anyone possessing a valid private access link may access the associated watchlist according to that link's permissions.

You can delete a synced watchlist using its edit access key. Inactive synced watchlists are automatically removed after approximately one year.

Technical data

Our servers may temporarily process IP addresses and request information for security, rate limiting, and reliable operation.

We do not use this information to build individual user profiles.

The site also loads certain external resources, including Google Fonts. Those providers may receive ordinary technical request information.

Third-party services

Links to government websites, GitHub, and other external services are governed by their respective privacy policies.

Changes

We may update this policy as the project develops. The current version will be available on this website with its updated date.

Questions

For questions about the project or its privacy practices, visit our GitHub repository.
