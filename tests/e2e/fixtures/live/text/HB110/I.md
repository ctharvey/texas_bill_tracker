# HB 110 - Introduced

90R128 DNC-D

By: Rodríguez Ramos H.B. No. 110

A BILL TO BE ENTITLED

AN ACT

relating to the minimum wage.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:

### SECTION 1.

Section 62.051, Labor Code, is amended to read as follows:

Sec. 62.051. MINIMUM WAGE. Except as provided by Section 62.057, an employer shall pay to each employee <ins>not less than the greater of:</ins>

<ins>(1) $15 an hour; or</ins>

<ins>(2)</ins> the federal minimum wage under Section 6, Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 (29 U.S.C. Section 206).

### SECTION 2.

Section 62.151, Labor Code, is repealed.

### SECTION 3.

This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
