# HB 448 - Introduced

90R3900 CMO-D

By: Lalani H.B. No. 448

A BILL TO BE ENTITLED

AN ACT

relating to monitoring air contaminant emissions in certain counties.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:

### SECTION 1.

Subchapter B, Chapter 382, Health and Safety Code, is amended by adding Section 382.0162 to read as follows:

<ins>Sec. 382.0162. MONITORING OF AIR CONTAMINANTS IN CERTAIN COUNTIES. (a) This section applies only to a county that:</ins>

<ins>(1) is adjacent to a county with a population of 3.3 million or more; and</ins>

<ins>(2) contains a coal-fired electric generating facility.</ins>

<ins>(b) The commission shall install and maintain at least one air quality monitor in each county described by Subsection (a). The monitors required under this section must:</ins>

<ins>(1) meet federal reference method or federal equivalent method criteria approved by the United States Environmental Protection Agency;</ins>

<ins>(2) be operated in accordance with 40 C.F.R. Part 53; and</ins>

<ins>(3) at a minimum, monitor:</ins>

<ins>(A) ozone;</ins>

<ins>(B) respirable particulate matter with a diameter of less than 2.5 microns; and</ins>

<ins>(C) sulfur dioxide.</ins>

<ins>(c) At least one monitor must be located near each coal-fired electric generating facility located in each county described by Subsection (a).</ins>

<ins>(d) The commission shall make available in real time on its Internet website data collected from a monitor required under this section.</ins>

<ins>(e) The commission shall include each monitor installed under this section in the commission's annual monitoring network plan for each of the pollutants listed in Subsection (b)(3).</ins>

### SECTION 2.

This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
