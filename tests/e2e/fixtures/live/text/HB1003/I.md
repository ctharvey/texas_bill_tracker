# HB 1003 - Introduced

90R1858 GP-D

By: Cole H.B. No. 1003

A BILL TO BE ENTITLED

AN ACT

relating to customers eligible for a discount program provided by a toll project entity.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:

### SECTION 1.

The heading to Section 372.053, Transportation Code, is amended to read as follows:

Sec. 372.053. <ins>CUSTOMER</ins> \[<del>PEACE OFFICER AND VETERAN</del>\] DISCOUNT PROGRAM.

### SECTION 2.

Section 372.053(a-1), Transportation Code, is amended to read as follows:

(a-1) A program established under Subsection (a) must include free or discounted use of the entity's toll project by an electronic toll collection customer whose account relates to a vehicle<ins>:</ins>

<ins>(1)</ins> registered under Section 504.202, 504.2025, or 504.315(f) or (g)<ins>; or</ins>

<ins>(2) registered to a:</ins>

<ins>(A) paramedic;</ins>

<ins>(B) peace officer; or</ins>

<ins>(C) firefighter</ins>.

### SECTION 3.

This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
