# HB 1001 - Introduced

90R1865 MP-D

By: Cole H.B. No. 1001

A BILL TO BE ENTITLED

AN ACT

relating to a mental health leave policy for certain public safety employees.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:

### SECTION 1.

Section 614.015, Government Code, is amended to read as follows:

Sec. 614.015. MENTAL HEALTH LEAVE FOR <ins>PUBLIC SAFETY EMPLOYEES</ins> \[<del>PEACE OFFICERS AND TELECOMMUNICATORS</del>\]. (a) In this section<ins>, "public safety employee" means a peace officer, fire fighter, detention officer, county jailer, or emergency medical services employee of this state or a political subdivision of this state. The term includes a person</ins>\[<del>:</del>

\[<del>(1) "Law enforcement agency" means an agency of the state or of a political subdivision of the state authorized by law to employ peace officers.</del>

\[<del>(2) "Telecommunicator" means a person</del>\] authorized to act as a telecommunicator under Section 1701.405, Occupations Code<ins>, or a person acting in a similar capacity under Chapter 773, Health and Safety Code</ins>.

(b) Each <ins>entity</ins> \[<del>law enforcement agency, and each agency</del>\] of the state or of a political subdivision of the state that employs a <ins>public safety employee</ins> \[<del>full-time telecommunicator,</del>\] shall develop and adopt a policy allowing the use of mental health leave by the <ins>public safety employees</ins> \[<del>peace officers and full-time telecommunicators, as applicable,</del>\] employed by the <ins>entity</ins> \[<del>agency</del>\] who experience a traumatic event in the scope of that employment.

(c) A mental health leave policy adopted under this section must:

(1) provide clear and objective guidelines establishing the circumstances under which a <ins>public safety employee</ins> \[<del>peace officer or telecommunicator</del>\] is granted and may use mental health leave;

(2) entitle a <ins>public safety employee</ins> \[<del>peace officer or telecommunicator</del>\] to mental health leave without a deduction in salary or other compensation;

(3) enumerate the number of mental health leave days available to a <ins>public safety employee</ins> \[<del>peace officer or telecommunicator</del>\]; and

(4) detail the level of anonymity for a <ins>public safety employee</ins> \[<del>peace officer or telecommunicator</del>\] who takes mental health leave.

(d) A mental health leave policy adopted under this section may provide a list of mental health services available to <ins>public safety employees</ins> \[<del>peace officers and telecommunicators</del>\] in the area of the \[<del>law enforcement or</del>\] employing <ins>entity</ins> \[<del>agency</del>\].

### SECTION 2.

This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
