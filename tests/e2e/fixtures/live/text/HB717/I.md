# HB 717 - Introduced

90R3317 KRM-D

By: Landgraf H.B. No. 717

A BILL TO BE ENTITLED

AN ACT

relating to county eligibility for grant awards under the rural ambulance service grant program.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:

### SECTION 1.

Section 130.914(a)(3), Local Government Code, is amended to read as follows:

(3) "Qualified county" is a county that has a population of <ins>200,000</ins> \[<del>68,750</del>\] or less.

### SECTION 2.

Section 130.914(e), Local Government Code, is amended to read as follows:

(e) The comptroller may award a grant under this section only in the following applicable amount:

(1) for a county with a population of less than 10,000, an amount not to exceed $500,000; or

(2) for a county with a population between 10,000 and <ins>200,000</ins> \[<del>68,750</del>\], an amount not to exceed $350,000.

### SECTION 3.

This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
